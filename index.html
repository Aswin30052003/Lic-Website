Quick tips

Things worth knowing

Hover a card to flip it — a few things I tell every client early on.

Buy term cover early

Premiums are locked to your age at purchase — starting in your 20s or 30s can cost far less over the policy's life than waiting.

Keep nominee details updated

Marriage, a new child, or a change of address — update your nominee promptly so a claim is never delayed over paperwork.

Review cover yearly

A promotion, a new loan, or a growing family usually means your existing cover needs a top-up. I check this with you every year.

Never let a policy lapse

Missing the grace period can mean losing years of cover and paid premiums. Set a reminder, or let me track it for you.
