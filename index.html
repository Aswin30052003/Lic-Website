Quick tips

Things worth knowing

Hover a card to flip it — a few things I tell every client early on.

Buy cover early

Premiums are usually lower when you begin earlier. Starting sooner can reduce long-term cost and improve protection.

Keep nominee updated

After marriage, a child, or any family change, update your nominee details so claim settlement remains smooth.

Review cover yearly

If your income, loan burden, or family responsibilities change, your protection may need an update too.

Don't miss renewals

Timely premium payment keeps your policy active. I can help with reminders so you never lose protection by mistake.
